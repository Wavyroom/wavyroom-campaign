Before you order — prices, permits, site, warranty

Prices. Quoted in US dollars and current at the date of your written quote. Until an order is confirmed, prices may change with exchange rates, tariffs and material costs. Your written quote is what binds, not this page.

Permits. The size thresholds described on this page are general and change by municipality. We do not certify that your project is exempt and we do not act as your design professional. Your city’s determination governs.

Use. These are detached accessory buildings, not dwelling units and not ADUs. Local rules often restrict sleeping, cooking, plumbing or rental use in a structure of this class. Confirm permitted use for your address before you order.

Site readiness. Prices and schedules assume a level, cleared site with truck access. Grading, drainage, tree or fence removal, craning and long carries are not included.

Electrical. No power is run to the unit as supplied. All on-site electrical work is performed by a licensed California electrician under a separate permit and is quoted separately.

Lead time. “Within three months” is our target from confirmed order to completed assembly. Ocean freight, customs, permit review and weather are outside our control and can move the date.

Finishes. Timber is a natural material. Grain, colour and the weathering of charred cladding vary between units and over time, and are not defects.

Warranty. Structure and frame are warranted five years, workmanship and installation one year, from completion. The warranty excludes customer negligence, unauthorised modification, movement of ground or foundations not supplied by us, misuse, and damage from storm, flood, fire or other events beyond normal weather. Full terms are issued with your order.

Contact. “A specialist calls you within two business days” is our service target, not a guarantee. Leaving your details places no order and no obligation on either side.

Before you ask

You are not managing this yourself.

We work with a licensed California general contractor. They handle the delivery, the assembly and the local trades, so you have one number to call instead of five.

What does the whole thing cost, all in?

The price on this page covers the room itself and ocean freight to Los Angeles. On top of that come delivery to your yard, assembly, and the power run from your house.

We do not print numbers for those, and you should be careful with anyone who does. What they cost depends on how far you are from Los Angeles, whether a truck can reach the spot, how level your ground is and how far the electrical run is. A figure quoted before anyone has looked at your yard is a guess, and guesses move in one direction later.

Your specialist prices every line for your actual address and sends it in writing. Nothing is charged until you have it in front of you.

My yard is bare dirt. Do I need to pour concrete first?

Usually not. The room sits on adjustable footings that our crew levels before assembly, so most yards take it as they are. What decides it is the slope, the soil and how much the ground moves when it rains.

Send photos and rough measurements of the spot and we will tell you straight. If a pad is genuinely worth pouring, our GC partner quotes it; if it is not, we say so.

How does the power get there? Can I add air conditioning?

Running a circuit from your house to the room is licensed electrical work in California. Our GC partner's electrician does it, or you can use your own. Either way it is a separate line so you can see what you are paying for it.

Air conditioning is not part of the standard build. A mini-split is the usual answer, and whether your city allows one in a detached backyard structure depends on how they classify it. We check that for your address before you order, not after.

Do I need a permit?

Many California cities treat a detached structure under a certain size differently from an ADU. In California that line is 120 square feet, which is why both of our models sit at or under it. But cities read it differently and adding electrical usually moves it.

British Columbia exempts a detached, single-storey accessory building of 10 m² — about 108 square feet — from a building permit. Both of our Canadian models are 89 sq ft, so both sit under that line. The exemption covers the building permit only: zoning setbacks, lot coverage and height limits still apply, any wiring needs its own electrical permit, and a few municipalities ask for a notification form.

We do not guess and we do not promise you are exempt. Our GC partner looks up what your address requires and handles the filing if there is one. Any fee the city charges is yours and is shown separately.

I'm in an HOA community. Will they approve it?

That is your HOA's decision, not ours. What we can do is give you a submission that looks like a submission: drawings, dimensions, exterior finishes, siting and the structural basis, rather than a brochure.

Send us your HOA's guidelines with your enquiry and we will tell you exactly which of their requirements we can document for you.

Is sales tax included?

No. Prices are before sales tax. How much applies, and to which lines, depends on your delivery address and how the order is written, so we do not print a rate here that might be wrong for your street.

Your quote shows the tax as its own line.

What if something goes wrong after it is built?

The structure and the frame are warranted for five years. Workmanship and the installation are warranted for one year from the day it is finished. Our California GC partner handles the call-out, so you are not filing a claim across the Pacific.

Outside it: normal wear, weather damage, misuse or neglect, and anything changed or added by another trade after handover. The full terms come with your quote, before you pay anything.

Which models can I buy in the United States?

Two: The Studio and The Sauna, both on this page.

How long from order to a finished room in my yard?

Within three months of your order, in your yard and finished. That covers the build in our factory, ocean freight, local delivery and assembly. The part that happens on your property takes three days or less.

You get a dated schedule with your quote, once we know your address and which model you want.

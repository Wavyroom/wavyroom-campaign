Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Wavyroom pages where you can request pricing for a backyard room in the United States. It is operated by Spacewavy Co., Ltd., the manufacturer of the Wavyroom brand, in the Republic of Korea.

What we collect

When you use the form on this site, you give us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The city and ZIP code where the room would go

We also record which link or QR code you arrived from, the page address you submitted from, and the time of your submission, so we know which campaigns are working.

We do not collect payment card details on this site. Nothing is charged here.

Why we use it

  • To call or email you back with a quote for your address
  • To hold your early-bird price and apply your coupon when you order
  • To arrange delivery and installation if you go ahead
  • To understand which campaigns bring genuine enquiries

We do not use your details for anything you did not ask us about.

Who sees it

Your details are shared only with the people needed to answer you:

  • Our own team, in Korea and the United States
  • Our California general contractor partner, when a quote or a site visit needs them
  • Service providers that store or move the data for us, such as Google for form storage and Vercel for hosting this page

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

Where it goes

Spacewavy is a Korean company, so your details are transferred to and stored in the Republic of Korea as well as the United States. We use providers that handle the transfer under standard contractual protections.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we are in contact with you about a room, and afterwards only as long as we need them for accounting and legal obligations. Ask us to delete your record and we will.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct it
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Tell us to stop contacting you

Email support@spacewavy.com and we will act on it. You do not need to give a reason.

California residents: under the CCPA and CPRA you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it deleted, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in that law. Use the same email address above.

Security

Enquiries are stored in access-controlled systems and sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfect, and we will tell you if something goes wrong with your data.

Children

This site is for adults buying a building. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us details, email us and we will remove them.

Cookies

This page does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If you arrived from a QR code or an ad, the link itself tells us which campaign it was. That is recorded with your enquiry, not stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
